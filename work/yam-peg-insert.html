背景Background

精密插销（peg-in-hole）对亚毫米级对齐、接触力学建模与多视角视觉反馈极为敏感。项目基于 MuJoCo Menagerie 的 i2rt_yam 机械臂与 MjSpec 构建了可复现的精密插销仿真环境与训练评测闭环。 Precision peg-in-hole manipulation is highly sensitive to sub-millimeter alignment, contact dynamics, and multi-view visual feedback. Built on MuJoCo Menagerie's i2rt_yam arm and MjSpec, this project establishes a reproducible simulation benchmark and end-to-end training/evaluation loop.

目标Goal

提供稳定、高速的 6-DoF 精密插销专家数据采集管线（100% / 98% 级专家基准），并通过不同策略架构与时序集成（Temporal Ensembling）消融，定量评估模仿学习在高精度接触任务上的瓶颈与改进空间。 Provide a stable, high-speed 6-DoF expert data collection pipeline (98%-level oracle) and systematically benchmark imitation learning policies and Temporal Ensembling (TE) ablations on tight-tolerance contact-rich insertion.

我的贡献What I Built

  • 基于 MuJoCo MjSpec 程序化组装 i2rt_yam 机械臂、双视角相机与可调公差插销-孔座场景，并完成接触参数标定。 Programmatically assembled the i2rt_yam arm, dual-camera setup, and tolerance-configurable peg-and-hole scene via MuJoCo MjSpec with tuned contact physics.
  • 实现 6-DoF 逆运动学（IK）专家轨迹生成器，覆盖随机初始位姿与孔位扰动，在 50 次评估中达成 98% 成功率并生成公开数据集 dream0906/yam。 Implemented a 6-DoF IK oracle trajectory generator across randomized initial poses and hole perturbations, achieving 98% success over 50 eval episodes and publishing dataset dream0906/yam.
  • 搭建 LeRobot 策略训练与评测管线，完成 ACT、ACT + Temporal Ensembling（TE）及 Diffusion Policy 的对比消融实验。 Built the LeRobot training and evaluation pipeline to run controlled ablations comparing ACT, ACT + Temporal Ensembling (TE), and Diffusion Policy.

反思Reflection

时序集成（TE）能有效平滑高频动作抖动，从而大幅提升插销阶段的一次通过率；但纯视觉策略在最后 1–2 mm 的盲区卡滞仍受限于腕部视角遮挡与缺乏力/触觉反馈，这也是后续引入合规控制与触觉表征的重点方向。 Temporal Ensembling effectively smooths high-frequency action jitter and boosts insertion pass rates. However, pure vision policies still struggle with the final 1–2 mm due to wrist-view occlusion and lack of force/tactile feedback—motivating future work on compliant control and tactile representations.
